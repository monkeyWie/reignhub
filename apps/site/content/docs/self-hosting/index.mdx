---
title: Open-source self-hosting
description: Choose where devices pair and how encrypted connections reach them.
---

Your agents and projects run on your computer. The connection service pairs your devices, helps them find a direct path and can relay encrypted traffic. Every client uses the same service URL. Open its `/chat/` page to use a browser.

| Where you want to run it                           | What to install                                                                       | What to expose                                                             |
| -------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| On your own machine or VM                          | [Go server](/docs/self-hosting/go) with its built-in Web console and SQLite           | HTTPS for the console and signaling, TCP and UDP for STUN and native relay |
| In your Cloudflare account                         | [Cloudflare Worker](/docs/self-hosting/cloudflare) with D1 and Durable Objects | One HTTPS origin, including the encrypted WebSocket relay                  |
| With a separate public IP for STUN or native relay | [Additional Go node](/docs/self-hosting/probe) alongside either control service       | Only the TCP and UDP ports you advertise                                   |

### Connect your devices

1. Open the service URL and confirm the Web console loads.
2. Enter that same root URL in the desktop app on the computer running your agents.
3. Pair your phone or browser using the desktop's device code. Approve the request on the computer, then open a project and read a session.

The open-source service works without a ReignHub account. Hosting costs follow your infrastructure provider's rates. See [backups and upgrades](/docs/self-hosting/operations).
